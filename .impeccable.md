## Design Context

### Users
Prospective B2B buyers arrive from a sales/contact page seeking the right Deepline conversation. They need to submit only their name and work email, understand that their company is being verified, and receive a trustworthy route without waiting on a dashboard.

### Brand Personality
Calm, exacting, and quietly ambitious. The experience should convey competent human handoff, not automated lead scoring theater.

### Aesthetic Direction
Editorial light-mode contact page inspired by the spacious restraint of Granola's sales page, but with an original Deepline composition. It uses warm paper neutrals, crisp dark ink, deep moss as the action color, and one expressive routing diagram rather than a card-heavy SaaS dashboard.

### Design Principles
1. Ask for the minimum: full name and work email only.
2. Make live verification legible without pretending work has finished.
3. Give the routing result one clear human next step.
4. Prefer generous whitespace, typographic hierarchy, and evidence on demand over decorative UI.
5. Respect reduced motion and maintain strong contrast and visible focus.
